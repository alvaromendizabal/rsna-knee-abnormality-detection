# Original-language adjudication and promotion gate

No candidate targets from this pilot are approved for training. Software validity, verbatim quotes, prompt agreement and increased coverage do not establish report-label accuracy.

## Independent review

A qualified reviewer who understands the report language should review the original report **before seeing model outputs**. Assign each of the twelve targets one of the six documented assertion states, identify the exact supporting passage, and flag uncertain target definitions. A second independent review and adjudication should resolve disagreements when possible. These four purposively chosen reports test implementation; they are too few and not randomly selected to estimate reliable performance.

The pilot's private selection contains original text and cached lexical states; it must not be uploaded or used as a blinded review screen because it reveals the lexical state. Create a genuinely blinded annotation view for the next review batch rather than asking the reviewer to validate the model's answers. Do not display the teacher's quotes or labels until the independent labels are locked. Select a separate representative, language-stratified review batch before reporting sensitivity/precision, and report abstention and denominator coverage by target and language. Do not treat Unicode script as language identity.

## Specific mechanisms to inspect

Preserve the distinction between injury and postoperative history; tear and intrameniscal degeneration; compartment-specific OA and an unspecified cartilage defect; effusion and synovitis; contusion and nonspecific marrow edema; a Baker cyst and other cysts; explicit absence and silence. Verify negation scopes, coordinated anatomical lists, sections, temporality, hedging and contradictions in the original language.

## Decision record

Report invalid-format rate, quote-presence rate, human-adjudicated state accuracy/precision/recall where support permits, abstention, per-target class counts and original-language subgroup performance. Do not choose a teacher solely by how many binary labels it generates. Preserve failed outputs and rejected approaches in private research history.

The 58 expert studies and their four normalized-report overlaps remain sealed for this milestone. Any later decision to use some expert outcomes for development must be explicit, documented and separated from an untouched final assessment. Do not silently relabel the entire expert pool as validation and repeatedly optimize on it.
