# Research timeline and decision log

This document presents the project as a sequence of **questions, evidence, and decisions**.

## Phase 1 — data and validation foundation

### Question
Can model comparisons be made without scanner/entity leakage and without mixing external and internal evaluation settings?

### Decision
Use scanner-disjoint grouped development evidence while keeping external recorded performance separate.

## Phase 2 — targeted complementary modeling

### Question
Can difficult targets benefit from specialized residual signal?

### Decision
Retain a positive development challenger without generalizing one narrow gain into a universal policy.

## Phase 3 — representation-diversity screens

Fixed spatial bias: negative aggregate screen.

Frozen orthopedic foundation features: valid negative matched screen.

### Decision
Close both tested configurations.

## Phase 4 — parent-first reconstruction

### Question
Should new work attach to surrogate systems or the actual heterogeneous parent?

### Decision
Restore trained parent branches and preserve fixed fitted numerical aggregation/calibration. Engineering parity is not model improvement.

## Phase 5 — validation and acquisition lineage

### Result
The fully labeled audit rows had historical selection exposure, and the scoped mirror had incomplete series coverage.

### Decision
Do not invent untouched confirmation membership. Do not relabel partial-input predictions as complete.

## Phase 6 — DICOM geometry hypothesis

### Result
2,287 real headers, 77 series, 26 studies, and 93 decoded images produced zero geometry flags.

### Decision
Close this hypothesis for the inspected scope unless materially new data arrive.

## Phase 7 — anatomy qualification

### Question
Can a pretrained knee-anatomy route provide credible localization evidence?

### Result
Nine structures passed the reference gate with mean Dice above 0.91.

### Decision
Preserve anatomy as an enabling capability while keeping native transfer exploratory.

## Phase 8 — winner-technique audit

### Question
What proven medical-imaging capabilities remain missing after the parent already contains representation diversity, attention, pretraining, and ensemble structure?

### Result
Public-safe inventory:

- 9 competitions;
- 15 top-solution lineages;
- 37 technique families;
- 29 high-confidence transferable mechanisms.

### Decision
Replace ad hoc model invention with **research → gap analysis → scoring → ranking → controlled transfer experiment**.

## Phase 9 — ordered cross-slice context

### Stage 101
A bounded held-out screen showed positive context signal relative to a matched orderless control.

### Stage 102
Full-cohort scanner-grouped cross-fitting produced:

- Stage 84: **0.7943834**
- ordered context: **0.7978448**

### Decision
Retain the mechanism as a real development improvement.

## Phase 10 — inference qualification

### Stage 103 result
Full-cohort export parity and fixed cache canaries passed.

### Decision
Treat this as engineering qualification, not another predictive gain.

## Phase 11 — explicit neighbor-context extension

### Stage 104 result
Point estimate increased slightly to **0.7978946**, but the paired uncertainty interval crossed zero.

### Decision
Preserve the point estimate as inconclusive and close repeated micro-tuning of this exact configuration.

## Phase 12 — current frontier

The next private direction is prepared around a materially different winner-derived capability: leakage-safe training-side robustness to weak labels.

Prepared work is **not represented as completed** until a verified owner return exists.

## Research principles carried forward

- retain valid positive evidence;
- preserve valid negative and inconclusive results;
- do not rewrite historical evaluation boundaries;
- separate engineering qualification from predictive improvement;
- keep source/data identity part of model identity;
- require promotion and kill gates;
- reuse completed expensive work;
- import capabilities from strong prior work rather than copying code;
- rank missing capabilities before spending GPU time.
